[b][color=#00ff00][size=5]PAL INSIGHT: QUICK STACK 1.4.3 — START HERE[/size][/color][/b]

Press [b]F5[/b] inside your current base to sell configured items, then store eligible normal-backpack items.

Quick Stack is a standalone client-side UE4SS Lua mod. Pal Insight is optional.

[b]WHAT'S NEW IN 1.4.3[/b]

[list]
[*]Quick Stack no longer stops after only 1.5 seconds when current-base storage data is still loading on a multiplayer client. It now continues waiting within the existing bounded job timeout.
[*]Stopped notifications now include a short reason code to help diagnose any remaining failures.
[/list]

Dedicated-server player reports confirm a separate issue remains: workbench counts may stay stale. 1.4.3 removes one early readiness stop; it cannot change storage network relevancy.

[b]QUICK START[/b]

[list=1]
[*]Install the [url=https://github.com/Okaetsu/RE-UE4SS/releases/tag/experimental-palworld]experimental Palworld build of RE-UE4SS[/url].
[*]Download [b]Steam/Win64[/b] or [b]Game Pass/WinGDK[/b] for your game.
[*]Extract it into the game folder containing the [b]Pal[/b] directory.
[*]Enter a base and press [b]F5[/b].
[/list]

With current Pal Insight active, use [b]F6 > Extensions > Pal Insight: Quick Stack[/b] for settings. Without Pal Insight, F5 uses saved or default settings and text notifications; there is no standalone F6 panel.

[b]Use one installation channel only.[/b] Do not combine Nexus, CurseForge, or Workshop copies, or mix Win64 and WinGDK packages. Close Palworld before updating.

[b]AUTOMATIC SELLING[/b]

All four categories are off by default. Checked keep-list items and Inventory [b]Tab > R[/b] exclusions are never sold. High-value items initially allow all 9 items; the other lists initially keep everything.

Selling runs before storage. Allowing ignored items in storage never allows them to be sold.

F5 searches the current base for an available merchant and applies Noble and Fine Furs passives from party Pals to sale prices. If no merchant is found, sale items stay in the backpack by default; F6 Settings can allow them to follow normal storage rules instead.

[b]DEFAULT STORAGE RULES[/b]

[list]
[*]Only the normal backpack and current base are used.
[*]Ignored items stay in your backpack; ordinary storage may receive new item types.
[*]Guild Chests, small Incubators, and Medicine Rack priority are off.
[*]The 5 cakes use Breeding Farms, cold storage, then ordinary storage; never Pal Food Boxes.
[*]Other food uses Pal Food Boxes, cold storage, then ordinary storage.
[*]Pal Eggs use large Incubators only.
[*]Relics use Ancient Relic Recyclers only; each Recycler keeps 10 World Tree Holy Water.
[/list]

F6 Settings can change these rules. Medicine Rack priority covers 3 current medical supplies and falls back to ordinary storage. Use [b]Tab > R[/b] to keep food in your backpack. Large Incubators take priority; occupied small ones are skipped.

[b]RESULTS AND PAL INSIGHT[/b]

Choose [b]Automatic[/b], [b]Text only[/b], or [b]Result window[/b]. Automatic opens detailed results when F5 is used from the inventory and shows text elsewhere. Sold items are green, stored items blue, excluded items muted gray, and unfinished items yellow. [url=https://www.nexusmods.com/palworld/mods/4638]Current Pal Insight[/url] provides the in-game settings entry and detailed result window.

Settings are saved outside the mod folder and 0.1.x settings migrate automatically.

[b]PLATFORM STATUS[/b]

Steam single-player, all 17 interface languages, and current Pal Insight are supported. On dedicated-server clients, item moves work client-side only, but workbench counts may stay stale for distant storage. Installing Quick Stack on the server does not change this. Co-op and Game Pass runtime remain unverified.

[b]TROUBLESHOOTING[/b]

If F5 does nothing, confirm that you are inside a base, verify the shortcut and package, install only one copy, and inspect [b]UE4SS.log[/b].

If an item was not sold or stored, check its sale toggle, keep list, [b]Tab > R[/b], available current-base merchant, routing, filters, permissions, capacity, and backpack location.

If dedicated-server workbench counts stay stale after F5, items may already be moved. Approach storage once and reopen the workbench. A complete fix needs a separate server-side storage-relevancy mod; Quick Stack neither provides nor requires one.

[b]REPORTING A PROBLEM[/b]

Include platform, play mode, versions, settings, reproduction steps, and complete [b]UE4SS.log[/b]. For crashes, attach the original [b].dmp[/b] and runtime/crash XML.
